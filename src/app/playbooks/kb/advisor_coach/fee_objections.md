# Fee objections

## Fee structure
Our advisory fee is charged as a percentage of assets under management, billed quarterly in arrears. Published tiers are shown in the fee schedule. Fees cover planning, portfolio construction, rebalancing, tax-loss harvesting where it applies, and ongoing reviews.

## Say this
"That's a fair question. The fee is a percentage of what we manage, so it only grows if your assets grow. Let me show you exactly what you'd pay on your numbers and what you'd get for it, then you can judge whether it's worth it."

## Comparing costs
Compare the all-in cost: advisory fee plus fund expenses. Many funds in our lineup are low-cost index or institutional share classes. Do not compare our fee to a product's expense ratio alone; the advisory fee buys planning and behaviour coaching, which the expense ratio does not.

## Negotiation
Fee tiers can change with total household assets and the account mix. Confirm the schedule in writing before quoting any different rate. Never promise a discount that is not in the schedule.
