# Market timing fears

## Why it comes up
Prospects worry about investing at a high market or before a downturn. Their fear is often a real loss, not a forecast.

## Say this
"Nobody can reliably predict short-term moves, including us. What we can do is size the money to your timeline and plan for drops that will happen. Would it help to see how the plan handles a bad year?"

## Facts to use
- Staggered investing (dollar-cost averaging) spreads entry over time. It does not guarantee a better outcome and can lag a lump sum when markets rise.
- Time in the market has historically mattered more than timing it, but past performance does not predict future results.
- Losses are possible in any year. Name the drawdown the plan can tolerate.

## Compliance
Do not say the market will recover, or that waiting will reduce losses. Do not guarantee outcomes. See compliance_disclosures.md.
