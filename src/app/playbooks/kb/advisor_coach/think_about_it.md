# "I want to think about it"

## Why it comes up
Prospects often want time because the decision feels large, or because they have not yet agreed with a spouse or partner. It is usually about trust or clarity, not the product.

## Say this
"Absolutely, it's a big decision and you should feel sure. What specifically would you like to think through? If it's the approach, we can cover that now. If it's timing, we can agree on a date to reconnect."

## Useful questions
- Is there one part of the plan you are unsure about?
- Who else should be part of the decision, and would they join a follow-up?
- What would make it an easy yes next time?

## Do not
Do not create urgency with deadlines or limited offers. Do not promise that a price or allocation will be available later.
