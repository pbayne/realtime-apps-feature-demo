# Compliance: what you may and may not say

## Never
- Guarantee returns, outcomes, or that a strategy will beat the market.
- Describe any investment as "risk-free", "can't lose", or a "sure thing".
- Promise a specific fee discount that is not in the published schedule.

## Always
- Say that investing involves risk, including possible loss of principal.
- Say that past performance does not predict future results.
- Offer the firm's disclosure brochure before recommending an account or product.

## If a prospect asks "is it guaranteed?"
"No investment is guaranteed. Results depend on markets and on your choices. I'll walk you through the disclosures so you can see the risks in writing."

## Complaints
If a prospect mentions a complaint or a regulator, stop the pitch, note it verbatim, and follow the complaints procedure.
