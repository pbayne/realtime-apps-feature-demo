# Account types and minimums

## Overview
We open managed accounts for individuals, joint accounts, IRAs and rollovers from workplace plans. Minimums vary by service tier; confirm the current minimum in the service schedule before quoting.

## Rollovers
A rollover moves an old workplace retirement account into an IRA. Ask whether the old plan has any early-withdrawal or vesting considerations before moving money.

## Say this
"We can take a look at how your existing retirement accounts could be consolidated, and I'll confirm the minimums and timelines for your situation before you decide anything."

## Do not
Do not recommend a rollover without first comparing fees and investment options in the old plan.
