# Discovery: goals and time horizon

## Purpose
A good first meeting ends with the prospect's goals written down in their own words, each with a rough date and dollar figure.

## Good questions
- What do you want the money to do for you, and by when?
- Which goal matters most if things do not go to plan?
- Is retirement a fixed date, or a range of years?
- Are there college, housing or care costs in the next ten years?

## Say this
"Let's list the goals together. For each one, roughly when do you need the money, and how much would make it feel secure?"

## Gaps to watch
If the time horizon for any goal is missing, ask before discussing products. Mixed horizons usually need different risk levels.
