# Decision makers

## Why it matters
Money decisions often involve a spouse, partner, or family member. Advising one person and finding out later that the other person disagrees wastes the meeting and damages trust.

## Questions
- Who else is involved in decisions about this money?
- Does your spouse or partner know you are meeting with us today?
- Would they like to join the next meeting?

## Say this
"Since this affects both of you, I'd like to include them in the next conversation. Would a joint meeting next week work?"

## Record
Note each decision maker's role and whether they attended. Do not share one person's financial details with another without their consent.
