# Risk tolerance

## Purpose
Separate how much risk the prospect can afford from how much they are comfortable taking. Both matter. The lower of the two sets the ceiling.

## Scenario question
"Imagine the portfolio fell 20 percent over a year. What would you most likely do: sell, hold, or add money?"

## Say this
"Your answer tells me more than any questionnaire. If you would sell in a drop, we should build for a smoother ride, even if it means slower growth."

## Capacity versus tolerance
Capacity: could the plan survive a bad year without changing the goals? Tolerance: would the client stay invested? Record both before proposing an allocation.
