# Already have an advisor

## Say this
"That's good to hear that you've been thinking about it. I'd like to understand what your current advisor does well and where you feel something is missing. There's no need to make a change today."

## Questions to ask
- How often do you meet, and what do you get from those meetings?
- Do you have a written plan with goals and a timeline?
- Who handles taxes and estate planning?
- What do you pay in total, including fund costs?

## Handling a second opinion
Position yourself as a complement, not a replacement, if that fits. Offer a one-time plan review. Do not disparage the other advisor.
