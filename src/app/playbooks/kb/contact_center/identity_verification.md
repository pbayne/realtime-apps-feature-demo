# Identity verification

## Standard checks
Verify two items: full name and date of birth, or postcode plus the last four digits of the account number. Never read out a full card number, bank number, or national identifier.

## Say this
"Before I can discuss the account, I need to confirm a couple of details. Could you give me your date of birth and the postcode on the account?"

## Failed verification
Do not share account details. Offer a call back to the number on file or a secure link.

## Sensitive data
If a customer reads out a full identifier, stop them, do not repeat it, and use the secure step instead.
