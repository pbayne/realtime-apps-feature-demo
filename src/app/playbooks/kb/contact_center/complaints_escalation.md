# Complaints and escalation

## What counts
Any expression of dissatisfaction that needs a response, plus any mention of a regulator, ombudsman, solicitor or lawyer, counts as a complaint.

## Say this
"Thank you for telling me. I'm going to log this formally so it gets the right review, and you'll receive a written acknowledgement within the timeframe we publish."

## Steps
1. Acknowledge without arguing.
2. Record the complaint verbatim with the date and account.
3. Give the reference number and the complaints timeline.
4. Do not offer compensation unless the complaints policy allows it at this stage.

## Legal threats
If the customer mentions legal action, log the complaint and notify the complaints team the same day.
