# Billing disputes

## Process
Confirm the disputed amount, the date and the account. Check for duplicate posting and pending adjustments. If the charge is wrong, reverse it under the adjustment policy and confirm the reference number.

## Say this
"I'm sorry you were charged that. Let me check the account now. If it's an error, I can reverse it today and you'll see the reference number in the confirmation."

## Timelines
Reversals post within five to ten business days. Customers may see a pending item for the same period. Do not promise a faster timeline.

## Escalate
Escalate to the billing team if the amount is over the authority limit or if the dispute has been raised twice without resolution.
