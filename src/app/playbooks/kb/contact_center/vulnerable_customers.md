# Vulnerable customers

## Signals
Bereavement, serious illness, job loss, mental-health crisis, cognitive difficulty, or a customer who sounds confused or unable to follow the call.

## Say this
"Thank you for sharing that. There's no rush. I can connect you with our support team who are trained for situations like this, and nothing has to be decided today."

## Actions
- Slow the pace and check understanding.
- Offer the vulnerable-customer team and record the flag on the account.
- Pause collections and avoid repeated call-backs.

## Do not
Do not pressure a payment or upsell during the call.
