# Cancellation and retention

## Process
Find out the real reason for cancelling before offering anything. Common reasons: price, service issue, moving, or a competitor offer.

## Say this
"I'm sorry to hear you're thinking of leaving. Can you tell me what's driven that? If it's the price, I can check the plans we have that might fit better."

## Offers (within authority)
- A plan downgrade that keeps the core service.
- One month at a reduced rate for a documented service failure.
- A pause of up to three months for relocation or temporary hardship.

## Do not
Do not describe a trial or offer as risk-free unless the terms say so. Do not delay cancellation if the customer confirms they want it after the offer.
