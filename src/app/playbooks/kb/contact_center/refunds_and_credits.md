# Refunds and credits

## Authority
Agents can issue goodwill credits up to the published limit for a documented service failure. Refunds to the original payment method need a reference and usually take five to ten business days.

## Say this
"I can apply a credit to your account today for the inconvenience. For the refund itself, you'll get a reference number and an email when it's processed."

## Do not
Do not promise a refund amount or a date before the team confirms it. Do not say a refund is guaranteed.
