# Payment plans and hardship

## Options
Offer a payment arrangement of up to three months for a short-term cash-flow problem. Longer plans need supervisor approval and a financial assessment.

## Say this
"Let's find an amount that works for you. I can set up a payment plan starting this month, and I'll send the terms by email so you have them in writing."

## Hardship
If the customer mentions losing a job, illness or a bereavement, switch to the vulnerable-customer process. Pause collection activity while the review runs.

## Do not
Do not demand a payment today. Do not describe late fees as unavoidable without checking the waiver policy.
